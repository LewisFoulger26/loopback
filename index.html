Help
what each button does
Planning
📍
My location — sets your start/finish point from GPS. You can also just tap anywhere on the map.
⛔
Avoid roads — toggles avoid mode: tap any road you don't want to run down and a red marker appears; regenerate to apply. Tap a marker again to remove it.
⛰
Terrain — "as flat as possible" or "give me hills" makes the generator compare candidate loops by real elevation data and pick accordingly. The route's total climb is shown either way.
✦
Generate route — builds a loop matching your distance within the chosen tolerance, starting and finishing at your start point. Quieter streets are automatically preferred over A- and B-roads, and the summary flags how much of the loop is on main roads.
↻
Try another — re-rolls the loop in a different direction if you fancy different scenery.
▶
Start run — begins sat-nav guidance. A large arrow shows which way the next corner goes, with a live countdown that turns amber as you get close, and a small dot marks the junction on the map. When two turns come in quick succession a "then" preview shows the one after. Everything is also spoken as you approach. Drift off course and it automatically routes you back (orange dashed line). The screen stays awake during a run.
🏃
My runs — your saved history on this device, topped by your totals and records: this week's distance, all-time distance, best pace and longest run (simulated runs don't count). 👁 shows that run's route on the map; ⬇ downloads it as a GPX file you can import into Strava or Garmin; 🗑 deletes it (asks first).
⚙
Settings — choose your units (km/metres or miles/yards — affects distances, turn callouts, pace and split announcements), and optionally paste a free openrouteservice.org API key to switch to proper walking routes with hard road avoidance.
During a run
🔊
Voice — mutes or unmutes the spoken directions and kilometre splits.
🧪
Simulate — previews the whole guided run with a phantom runner, no GPS needed. Saved simulated runs are tagged sim.
■
End run — stops the run and shows your distance, time, pace and climb, with the option to save or discard.
On your phone
📲
Install as an app — open the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen (Android: Chrome menu → "Install app"; iPhone: Share → "Add to Home Screen"). LoopBack then opens full-screen like a normal app, and the app itself loads even without signal — though the map, route generation and elevation data still need a connection.
🔆
During a run the screen stays awake so GPS guidance isn't cut off, and each kilometre is announced with your average pace.
